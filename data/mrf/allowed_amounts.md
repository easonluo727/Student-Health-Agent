## Data notes

- **Negotiated rate ≠ final allowed amount.** MRF rates are contract prices before claim edits, bundling, multiple-procedure reductions, outlier payments, and member cost sharing. Treat them as the best public estimate of the allowed amount, not a quote.
- **Basis order:** `provider_rate` (NPI requested) → `ucla_health_rate` (UCLA Health NPIs/TINs in Tier A) → `westwood_area_median` (Tier A ZIPs 90024, 90025, 90095, 90049, 90064, 90067) → `la_county_median` (Tier B: ZCTAs ≥50% inside LA County) → `rate_unavailable`. Medians are over per-provider values.
- **Per-provider value:** dollar types only (`negotiated`, `derived`, `fee schedule`); `percentage` and `per diem` are excluded and counted in `n_rates_excluded` together with expired rates; POS-specific rate preferred, else a rate with no POS (or CMS `CSTM-00` = all POS); latest expiration date; median if a provider still has several values.
- **Imaging:** global rate, else TC + 26 summed per provider.
- **UC SHIP** is self-funded and uses Anthem Blue Cross Prudent Buyer PPO network rates; the Anthem rows are the demo's prices.
- **Medicare benchmarks are not used** anywhere in this table.
- **Snapshot:** one monthly MRF release; rates change monthly.
