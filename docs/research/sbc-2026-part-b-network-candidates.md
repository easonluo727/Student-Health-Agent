## Network 9 — UnitedHealthcare "Choice Plus"

Note: UnitedHealthcare does not sell individual-market plans on Covered California, so no California individual-market candidate exists. The purchasable Choice Plus products open to US students are university student health plans (UnitedHealthcare StudentResources).

### Candidate 9.1 — Saint Mary's College of California (California ✅)
- **Plan name**: 2026 – 2027 Student Health Insurance Plan: Saint Mary's College of California
- **Insurer**: UnitedHealthcare Insurance Company (via UnitedHealthcare StudentResources)
- **Who can buy**: All full-time **domestic** undergraduate students at the college are automatically enrolled (per the plan summary hosted on the school's site: "All Full-Time Domestic Undergraduate students … are automatically enrolled"). Open to US citizens/residents; not an international-student plan.
- **Plan year**: 2026–2027
- **Network**: Choice Plus (plan summary flyer: "Find an in-network provider: Choice Plus")
- **Official pages**:
  - School-hosted plan summary: https://www.stmarys-ca.edu/sites/default/files/2026-06/Summary%20of%20Benefits%20-%202026-2027.pdf
  - UHCSR school portal: https://studentcenter.uhcsr.com/stmarysca
- **SBC direct link**: not found
- School is in Moraga, CA.

## Network 10 — Cigna "National PPO" (Open Access Plus / PPO)

**No California candidate found.** Key findings:
- Cigna's 2026 Individual & Family market covers only 11 states (AZ, CO, FL, GA, IL, IN, MS, NC, TN, TX, VA) — California is not one of them, and Cigna exits the individual market nationwide after 2026 (existing plans run through 2026-12-31).
- Cigna's own 2026 IFP SBCs located on cigna.com (e.g., FL "Partnered Care Gold-1", IL "Connect Preferred Gold Rx Copay") are all Plan Type **HMO**, not Open Access Plus.
- Open Access Plus is essentially an employer-group product; the only individually purchasable OAP products are university student health plans (SHIPs), administered for Cigna by Wellfleet Student.

### Candidate 10.1 — Wesleyan University (Connecticut ⚠️ not California)
- **Plan name**: WESLEYAN UNIVERSITY 2026 – 2027 STUDENT HEALTH INSURANCE PLAN
- **Insurer**: Cigna (administered by Wellfleet Student)
- **Who can buy**: All full-time graduate and undergraduate students, auto-enrolled on a waiver basis (2026–27 premium $3,267; waiver deadline 2026-08-19). Open to US students; not international-only.
- **Plan year**: 2026–2027
- **Network**: Cigna Open Access Plus (OAP) — official benefit guide: "PPO Network: Cigna Open Access Plus (OAP)"
- **Official pages**:
  - http://www.wesleyan.edu/healthservices/insurance/
  - Benefit guide (school-hosted): https://wesleyan.edu/healthservices/Images/2026%202027%20Wesleyan%20University%20Benefit%20Guide.pdf
- **SBC direct link**: not found

### Candidate 10.2 — Trinity University, Texas (⚠️ not California)
- **Plan name**: TRINITY UNIVERSITY 2026 – 2027 STUDENT HEALTH INSURANCE PLAN
- **Insurer**: Cigna (administered by Wellfleet Student; enrollment/waiver via Academic HealthPlans)
- **Who can buy**: All undergraduates taking 9+ credit hours and all full-time graduate students, auto-enrolled at registration (waiver window 07/08/2026–08/31/2026). Open to US students; not international-only.
- **Plan year**: 2026–2027
- **Network**: Cigna Open Access Plus (plan materials: "Access to a broad network from Cigna Open Access Plus")
- **Official pages**:
  - Plan portal: https://trinity.myahpcare.com/ (AHP is the plan administrator; no standalone Trinity .edu insurance page was found)
  - Benefits at a Glance: https://myahpcare.com/wp-content/uploads/WEB-2627-Trinity-University-TX-BGlance-7.28.26-RC.pdf
- **SBC direct link**: not found

Excluded near-misses for network 10: Missouri Baptist University 2026–27 (requires F-1/J-1 visa — international-only, fails the hard condition); City of San Diego 2026 Cigna OAP and other employer OAP SBCs (employer plans, not purchasable by individuals); Stanford Cardinal Care 2026–27 (uses Blue Shield of California PPO in CA, Cigna PPO only outside CA, and not OAP); University of San Diego SHIP (2026–27 carrier is Aetna).
